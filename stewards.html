Stewards

Current Stewards

Stewards are participants entrusted by the community with specific responsibilities — moderation, domain curation, event coordination, archive maintenance, and more. One role, defined by mandate, earned through contribution.

All past stewardships are archived. The full participant directory is available to participants.

Active stewards

Stewardship mandates are bounded — they end when their purpose is concluded. The record of every past stewardship is preserved in the archive.
