## Cells — Deep Clarification (2026-07-24, updated 2026-07-25)

### What is a Cell?
A Cell is a **deliberation/discussion/activity space** — think of it as a chatroom, but each one is unique in its purpose and membership. Cells are where work actually happens.

### Circle Home vs Circle Cell

**Circle Home** (accessible from circles list):
- The circle's public-facing page — profile, discussions, activity, membership, histories
- Shows: circle profile, restricted discussions, motions, resolutions, circle activity feed
- Discussions here are restricted to circle members
- Accessible from the circles list

**Circle Cell** (restricted, accessible from cells dashboard):
- A private work cell for circle members, like a project cell
- Where proposals originate, xSTFs are created, deliverables are produced
- Has: proposals list, xSTFs list, deliverables, cell messages
- Activities in the circle cell appear in the circle's activity feed
- Circle cells are listed in the circle's activities

### Cell Types and Their Lifecycle

**1. Circle Cell**
- Restricted work cell for circle members
- Where proposals originate, xSTFs are created, deliverables are produced
- Members: circle stewards + mandated domain participants
- Purpose: originate proposals, create xSTFs, review deliverables
- Activities here feed into the circle's activity feed

**2. Deliberation Cell**
The core governance mechanism. A deliberation cell has four components:

1. **Deliberation** — a chat thread where participants discuss
2. **Proposal** — the formal proposal being deliberated, with a link to the source discussion thread if any (or report message(s) for jSTF, or complaint for jSTF)
3. **Deliverable** — the motion, action, or outcome produced by deliberation (always one: a motion to aSTF)
4. **Competence-weighted vote** — when all vote and the motion passes, the cell creates/opens an aSTF

**Layout:** layout-split (1fr 300px sidebar). Main content = deliberation feed + vote. Sidebar = participating circles, guide, draft resolutions, conditional actions, system settings.

**Proposal Origins (how a proposal enters a deliberation cell):**
1. **Discussion thread → steward proposal**: A user creates a discussion thread, participants discuss, and a steward creates a proposal from that thread. The proposal links back to the source thread.
2. **Direct steward proposal**: A steward directly creates a proposal without a preceding discussion thread.
3. **System-bound proposal**: A steward makes changes to a system setting or circle profile, submits the changes as a proposal. When the resolution passes, the approved system settings are activated automatically.

**Multi-circle participation:**
Deliberation cells can have multiple circles participating, not just the originating circle.

**Self-disqualification:**
Circles can self-disqualify from a deliberation. This is an auditable decision — the circle appears as a "ghost/scar" in the participating circles list, showing "withdrew from deliberation by: [member name]" to indicate which member made the withdrawal decision. The scar is permanent and visible to auditors.

### Resolution Drafting

Resolutions are the recorded outcomes of deliberation. A resolution is the discussed decision that is voted on — not the original proposal. The proposal is the starting point; resolutions are drafted by participants based on deliberation.

- The first resolution draft is the proposal text verbatim
- Participants can edit the resolution or add additional resolutions
- Each edit nullifies existing votes (must re-vote)
- The resolution type (Directive / System / Hybrid) is set in the drafting modal
- Multiple resolutions can exist in one deliberation cell, each spawns its own aSTF
- A failed resolution is still recorded as a failed resolution (not discarded)

**Resolution drafting modal** (full overlay):
- Resolution text (editable textarea)
- Action field (free text with autofill presets: Community Policy, Declaration, Deliverable Document, Service Contract, Agreement, Letter/Communication) + implementing circle(s) selector
- Vote control: Yea / Nay / Abstain buttons + per-domain vote breakdown table
- Save button (nullifies votes on save)

Resolution type is implicit — set at the deliberation cell level by the trigger source, never shown to users. Circle/Settings edits produce system-bound proposals. Direct steward proposals are directives.

### Accept Record Checkbox

Before voting, participants must check "I confirm this discussion record accurately reflects the deliberation." This checkbox is inside the resolution modal (not in the main view). Each time the modal is opened, the checkbox starts unchecked and vote buttons are disabled. The checkbox ensures participants agree the deliberation record is complete and accurate before voting on that specific resolution.

### Deliberation Cell Voting Mechanics

**Three vote options:** Abstain, Nay, Yea

**Per-member vote weight:**
Each member's vote weight = their Ws (soft competence) intersection with the deliberation domains. If a member has 2800 Ws in Xenobiology but the resolution is about Space Law and Liability, their Xenobiology Ws contribute zero influence. Their Veterinary Biology Ws (200) may be significant if the resolution touches that domain.

**Per-domain vote breakdown:**
For each domain the resolution covers, the system shows:
- Domain name
- Yea Ws total (sum of all yea voters' Ws in that domain)
- Nay Ws total (sum of all nay voters' Ws in that domain)
- Total Ws in that domain
- Individual voter contributions (avatar, Ws, vote direction)

**Aggregate vote:**
The final vote is an aggregate of all stewards on all intersecting domains with the resolution, across all domains of the resolution. Shown as total Yea Ws vs total Nay Ws.

**Decision Status Bar:**
Shown below the vote table. Shows quorum status (e.g., "Quorum: 4/7"), vote result (e.g., "Yea 4,960 · Nay 932 (passes)"), and two buttons:
- **Submit Resolution** — green, active when quorum met + passes, submits to aSTF
- **Close Debate** — red, always active, anyone can press to crystallise the cell. Resolution recorded as final outcome.

### System Settings Modal (tabbed)

Opens as a full overlay. Four tabs:
1. **Circle** — circle name, status, description, max members
2. **STF** — min/max vSTF assessors, aSTF assessors, STF durations (days)
3. **Quorum** — minimum participants, minimum % of members, pass threshold (% yea), abstain limit (%)
4. **Deliberation** — max deliberation days, auto-close inactivity, max resolutions per cell, vote nullify on edit

Submit creates a system-bound proposal to the deliberation cell.
Also accessible from Circle Home sidebar as "Circle Settings".

**3. Project Cell**
- Projects can have their own cells for deliberation
- Project cells may have different membership rules than circle cells
- Spawn deliverables (unlike circle cells which originate proposals)

**4. Motion → aSTF (Adjudication STF)**
- A motion from deliberation spawns an **aSTF** (Adjudication Special Task Force)
- aSTF operates in an **isolated blind cell** — members don't know each other
- aSTF audits the motion in isolation
- aSTF verdict: **Resolution** (motion approved) or **Rejection** (motion denied)

**5. vSTF / jSTF / Periodic Review aSTF**
- All operate in **isolated blind cells**
- **vSTF**: Verification STF — evaluates competence claims, evidence
- **jSTF**: Investigation STF — investigates specific issues (see jSTF section below)
- **Periodic Review aSTF**: Reviews overall health of a circle and its stewards
- All produce **evaluations** (not resolution/rejection)

**6. xSTF → Deliverable**
- xSTF spawns to **execute** a resolved motion
- Produces a **deliverable** per requirements from the **commissioning circle** (the stewards' cell that requested it)

### jSTF — Judicial Investigation Cell

**Trigger:**
Any member can report any member. The report is surfaced as an **anonymous post to the Commons**, visible to stewards. Any steward can sponsor the report, which activates a jSTF.

**jSTF Deliberation:**
The jSTF deliberates on the report in a blind cell. During deliberation, the jSTF can:

1. **Restrict reported user activity** — e.g., suspend steward privileges, limit participation in certain cells, during the investigation period
2. **Select punitive action** if warranted — based on policy resolutions previously passed by the Commons (cited as precedent)
3. **Push a disciplinary motion** — which goes to aSTF for audit

**aSTF Audit of jSTF Disciplinary Motion:**
- aSTF audits the **decision**, not the members
- aSTF **never sees jSTF members** — complete separation
- aSTF reviews whether the disciplinary action is proportionate and consistent with Commons policy

**Resolution:**
When the disciplinary motion passes aSTF audit, the resolution can be:

1. **System-bound**: Remove from circle, trigger fresh vSTF, freeze Ws for a set time, among others
2. **Non-system disciplinary**: Applied as per policy resolutions passed before, cited as precedent

### Flow Summary

```
Discussion → Proposal (steward-sponsored) → Deliberation Cell (layout-split with sidebar)
                                                          ↓
                                              Accept record → Draft Resolution(s)
                                              Edit/vote in modal → Per-domain vote table
                                              Yea Ws vs Nay Ws per domain + aggregate
                                                          ↓
                                              If passes: Submit Resolution → aSTF (blind cell)
                                              If fails: Close Debate → Failed resolution recorded
                                                          ↓
                                              aSTF verdict: Resolution / Rejection
                                              → opens xSTF → Deliverable
                                              (each resolution spawns its own aSTF)

jSTF: Report → Anonymous post → Steward sponsors → jSTF Cell (blind)
                                                          ↓
                                                   Activity restriction (if needed)
                                                          ↓
                                                   Disciplinary resolution → aSTF audit
                                                          ↓
                                                   System-bound / Non-system resolution

Periodic Review aSTF → Evaluates circle health + steward performance
vSTF → Evaluates competence claims
xSTF → Executes resolved resolutions → Deliverable

Circle Cell → originates proposals, creates xSTFs, reviews deliverables
              activities appear in circle's activity feed

Circle Home → circle profile, discussions, resolutions, activity, membership, settings
              public-facing page accessible from circles list
```

### Key Principles
- **Blind isolation**: STF cells (aSTF, vSTF, jSTF, xSTF) operate blind — members don't know each other's identities during deliberation
- **Steward sponsorship required**: Not just anyone can propose — a steward must sponsor it
- **Domain mandate boundaries**: Cells only operate within the domains the responsible circle has mandate over
- **Each cell is unique**: Different membership, different purpose, different rules
- **Anonymous reporting**: jSTF reports are anonymous to protect reporters; sponsors are stewards who take responsibility
- **Proportionate discipline**: jSTF can restrict activity during investigation but must cite policy precedent for punitive action
- **aSTF independence**: aSTF never sees jSTF members — audits the decision, not the people
- **Domain intersection voting**: Deliberation votes are weighted by member Ws in the specific domains the motion covers, not total Ws
- **Self-disqualification scars**: Circles that withdraw from deliberations leave permanent auditable ghosts/scars
