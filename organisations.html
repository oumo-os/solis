Affiliated organisations

Organisations affiliate with the Commons — they do not join it. Each retains its own governance, identity, and independence entirely.

Affiliation means participation and visibility. Nothing more, nothing less.

Does your organisation belong here?

If your organisation engages with humanity's future beyond Earth — in any discipline — you're welcome to affiliate. No application, no approval, no fees.
