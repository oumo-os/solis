Deliberation Guide
A deliberation cell is a focused discussion space where participants draft and vote on resolutions.
Proposals enter via discussion threads, direct steward action, or system-bound edits.
Resolutions pass to aSTF for blind adjudication. Failed resolutions are still recorded.
Flow: Proposal → Deliberation → Draft Resolution(s) → Vote → aSTF / Record
